Studying the geometry of learning in quantum systems.

I am Marwan Ait Haddou, a PhD researcher working at the intersection of quantum computing, machine learning, and information geometry. My work focuses on how parameterized quantum models learn, what they can access through measurement, and how their geometry shapes optimization.

01 / About

Research driven by structure, not just benchmarks.

I am interested in the mathematical structure behind quantum machine learning: tangent spaces, information geometry, effective dimension, measurement-induced constraints, and the optimization of variational quantum circuits.

My approach combines theoretical reasoning with reproducible numerical experiments. I aim to build methods and diagnostics that explain why a model behaves the way it does, rather than reporting a downstream score alone.
