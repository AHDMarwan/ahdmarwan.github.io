01 / ABOUT

Research driven by structure, not only performance.

I am interested in what makes a quantum model learnable: which directions are visible to a measurement, which geometric structures govern optimization, and how much of a model's nominal parameter space is actually accessible to learning.

My work combines numerical experiments, scientific computing, and tools from quantum information and differential geometry. I aim to build research artifacts that are reproducible, falsifiable, and useful beyond a single benchmark.

Accessible Quantum Natural Gradient ↗

A PennyLane implementation of measurement-aware natural-gradient optimization using accessible Fisher geometry, with primal/dual solvers, metric caching, adaptive refresh, and trust-region safeguards.

Spectral Geometry of Accessible Quantum Tangents ↗

A rigorous, outcome-blind experiment suite studying the spectral structure of accessible quantum tangent spaces, with frozen protocols, technical validation, family-balanced analysis, and finite-size diagnostics.

A study of whether PQC architectures organize into stable low-dimensional descriptor spaces after controlling for circuit size and complexity, using PCA, UMAP, confounding analysis, and architecture-level comparisons.
